Privacy notice

Last updated 10 October 2026

Who we are

RunwayCheck is a trading name of a sole trader based in the UK. The owner’s full legal name and UK business address will be added here before we take any payment. RunwayCheck offers a free cash runway calculator and optional cashflow planning sessions for UK freelancers and small company directors. We are the “controller” (the organisation responsible) for the personal data described here. For anything here, email localreply@agentmail.to.

The calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The figures you type are never sent to us or stored by us.

What we collect

  • If you contact us: your name, business name, email address and your message.
  • If you book a session or check-in: your contact details, the figures you choose to share (cash, money in, money out, known tax bills), the 13-week cash sheet we fill in together and the written summary we send you.
  • Payments, once open, are handled by Stripe, which tells us your name, email, billing details, the last four digits of your card and whether payment succeeded. We never see your full card number.

Money can be a stressful subject. We use what you tell us only to run your session. If you tell us you are in financial difficulty, we will use that only to point you to free help (such as Business Debtline or MoneyHelper), never to sell you anything.

Our inbox

Emails to localreply@agentmail.to go to a shared, receive-only inbox that the same owner uses for a few small businesses. The inbox is stored by our provider, AgentMail, which hosts our email inbox. We use your message only to answer you about RunwayCheck, and we keep it for up to 12 months after our last contact.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers. Fonts are served from our own site. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • Replying to enquiries: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Running your session or check-in and sending your sheet and summary: performance of our contract with you.
  • Taking payment and preventing fraud: contract, and our and Stripe’s legitimate interests.
  • Keeping business and tax records: legal obligation.

We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe, for payments (once payments open);
  • our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.).

The email and video-call providers we use for sessions will be listed here before sessions open.

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States. We are confirming the contract terms and the legal basis each provider uses to protect personal data sent outside the UK. This section will name them once confirmed. You can ask us for details at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries: up to 12 months after our last contact. Session figures, sheets and summaries: the retention period will be set out here before sessions open. You keep your own copy of the sheet. Payment records: for as long as tax law requires (at least 5 years after the 31 January tax-return deadline for the year concerned).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email us and we will respond within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first by emailing us at localreply@agentmail.to with “Data complaint” in the subject line. We will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
