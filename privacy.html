Privacy notice

Last updated 14 October 2026

Who we are

Operated by a UK sole trader trading as RunwayCheck. Contact: evanturner557+runwaycheck@gmail.com. Postal address available on written request. RunwayCheck offers a free cash runway calculator and optional cashflow planning sessions for UK freelancers and small company directors. We are the “controller” (the organisation responsible) for the personal data described here. For anything here, email evanturner557+runwaycheck@gmail.com.

The calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The figures you type are never sent to us or stored by us.

What we collect

  • If you contact us: your name, business name, email address and your message.
  • If you book a session or check-in: your contact details, the figures you choose to share (cash, money in, money out, known tax bills), the 13-week cash sheet we fill in together and the written summary we send you.
  • Payments, once open, are handled by Stripe, which tells us your name, email, billing details, the last four digits of your card and whether payment succeeded. We never see your full card number.

Money can be a stressful subject. We use what you tell us only to run your session. If you tell us you are in financial difficulty, we will use that only to point you to free help (such as Business Debtline or MoneyHelper), never to sell you anything.

Our inbox

Emails to evanturner557+runwaycheck@gmail.com go to the owner's Gmail inbox via a plus-address used only for RunwayCheck. Google provides the mailbox. We use your message only to deal with your enquiry about RunwayCheck, and we keep it for up to 12 months after our last contact.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers. Fonts are served from our own site. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • Replying to enquiries: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Running your session or check-in and sending your sheet and summary: performance of our contract with you.
  • Taking payment and preventing fraud: contract, and our and Stripe’s legitimate interests.
  • Keeping business and tax records: legal obligation.

We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Google (Gmail), which hosts our email inbox;
  • Stripe, for payments (once payments open);
  • our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.).

Google and GitHub are based in the United States, so personal data we share with them may be stored or processed there. GitHub handles our website host’s visitor logs under its own privacy statement. GitHub relies on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the ‘UK–US data bridge’) for transfers from the UK (see GitHub’s privacy statement). Emails sent to us are handled under Google’s privacy policy. Please don’t send us sensitive personal information by email. You can ask us for details at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to an order: up to 12 months after our last contact. Session details (the figures you share and our copies of your sheet and summary) are deleted 30 days after we send your summary; you keep your own copy. We keep only a basic order record (your name, email, date, what you bought and the amount) for as long as tax law requires (at least 5 years after the 31 January tax-return deadline for the year concerned).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email us to ask. Requests sent to our inbox are handled by the owner (not by automatic replies) within one month, as the law requires.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first by emailing us at evanturner557+runwaycheck@gmail.com with “Data complaint” in the subject line. The owner will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
